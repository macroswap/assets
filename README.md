# MacroSwap brand assets

Official logos and icons of **MacroSwap**, the DAW (Decentralized Autonomous Wallet) that buys the Bitcoin dip on autopilot.

> **Buy the dip. On autopilot.**

The mark is an "M" drawn as a chart: two peaks with the **dip** in the middle, and the amber dot is the buy.

| File | Use | Background |

## Typography

- Headings and wordmark: **Space Grotesk** 600. The wordmark is **macro**swap, with "swap" in amber.
- Body: **Inter** 400/500.
- Numbers and addresses: **JetBrains Mono**.

All three are free on Google Fonts.

## Usage

**Do**
- Keep clear space around the mark at least the height of the amber dot.
- Use the light mark on light backgrounds and the regular mark on dark ones, or the icon (works on both).
- Link to the official site: **https://macroswap.finance**.

**Don't**
- Recolor, stretch, rotate, add effects or rebuild the mark.
- Use the logo in a way that suggests a partnership, an endorsement or an official MacroSwap product that isn't one.
- Use it on sites, apps or tokens that imitate MacroSwap. MacroSwap has **no token**: anything sold as "the MacroSwap
  token" is not ours.

## About MacroSwap

MacroSwap is 100% DeFi: your own wallet follows rules you set on-chain, and open keepers execute them. Buy a BTC-led basket
when Bitcoin dips, DCA or buy and hold from a personal deposit address, and hedge back to stablecoins with an instant sell
or a profit target. Non-custodial, Chainlink prices, fixed fees. Not a promise of returns and not investment advice: crypto
assets are volatile and you can lose money.
